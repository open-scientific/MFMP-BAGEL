- The persistent E-Field is predicted by Nevessky and Zhvirblis for a 1,2,3,4-tor even order structure like this. Dave sees nothing (E or M) on Fe or non-Fe coils before power turned on, so no induction. In the case of Fe core tor he states he has seen a persistent though lower intensity E-Field in the original location, even when the switched off tor is moved to a new position - the tor carried part of the field with it. If he tries to build a persistent field on an ESD matt, it does not work. This supports the finding by John Hutchison that he saw no effects unless his samples were on an insulating base - such as Wood or plastic crate.
- D4D ratios important to ensure focus point is centered.
